B.R.I.M.

Barangay Resident Information and Management System

Digitizing and automating the entire resident registration and service request workflow for faster, smarter local governance.

Our Problem

Traditional, manual registration processes in barangays are often tedious, prone to human error, and inefficient. This results in long queues, missing documents, and delayed public services.

Our Solution

We digitize and automate the entire workflow, creating a centralized, secure, and easily accessible database for local government units, eliminating paper trails and administrative bottlenecks.

Target Users

Barangay Residents: Need an easy way to register, pay fees, and request services online.

Barangay Staff: Require an organized dashboard to verify, approve, and manage resident records efficiently.

System Workflow & Features

1

Resident Registration

A new resident visits the portal and fills out the registration form, which is processed by the Laravel backend.

2

Secure Profile Creation

The system creates a core User account using UUIDs for security and automatically assigns a "Resident" role via Spatie RBAC.

3

Onboarding Notifications

Laravel instantly sends a formatted HTML welcome email via Mailtrap and a welcome SMS through the Traccar gateway

4

Service & Payment Trigger

For documents or events, residents are redirected to a secure Stripe Checkout session to process required fees.

5

Cryptographic Confirmation

Stripe fires a signed webhook back to the server; B.R.I.M. verifies the signature and flags the record as "Paid".

6

Administrative Review

The request appears on the Secretary's dashboard for final verification and manual "Approve" triggering.

7

Make.com PDF Automation

Upon approval, Make.com catches the JSON payload, maps it to a Google Doc template, and generates an official PDF.

8

Document Issuance

The PDF is saved to Google Drive, and the resident receives an email notification that their document is ready.

9

Real-time Dispatch

The Traccar SMS gateway sends a specific tracking number or emergency alert directly to the resident or responder's phone.

10

Smart Ticket Generation

The system verifies event capacity and automatically emails a dynamically generated HTML ticket to the resident.
